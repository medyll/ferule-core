ℹ️ About Ferule-Core

            
            

                

                    
🌌 Matrix Topology

                    

                        Translates linear text intent into topological representations — probability clouds of meaning 
                        rather than word sequences. The sensory organ that perceives structure in conceptual space.
                    

                    
3 Levels:

                    
    
                        
  • Constellations: Macro intent — thematic domains
    • 
                        
  • Amass: Clusters of meaning in simultaneous orbit
    • 
                        
  • Semantic Quarks: Micro-level floating-point weights
    • 
                    

                

                
                

                    
🏛️ Structural Mapping

                    

                        Dynamic organizational power structure mapper. Generates indicators dynamically based on 
                        subject context using the nomenclature ind-[group]-[name5].
                    

                    
Nomenclature:

                    
    
                        
  • ind: Fixed prefix
    • 
                        
  • [group]: Category (3 letters)
    • 
                        
  • [name5]: Specific name (5 letters)
    •
